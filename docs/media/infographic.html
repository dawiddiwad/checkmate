Checkmate does not start, schedule, or message the agents. The factory does. The agents coordinate through the records: inbox, gap answer, plan revise, plan approve, run begin --retest-of, result accept, or a /checkmate:* skill. The oracle is anyone who can give the answer: a product-owner agent or a person.

Solo mode: you are the oracle

The same records, in your own Claude Code. Test your own work, or verify a teammate's.

> /checkmate:plan checkout from docs/checkout.md
> /checkmate:ui
> /checkmate:revise checkout
> /checkmate:execute checkout@2
