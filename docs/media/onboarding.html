Completion, outcomes, and coverage are separate fields. The caller branches on them, not on prose.
a failure has a finding
A failed check needs a defect and its evidence. A tool failure is an error instead.
result.json, report.md, and report.html are the same record.

Which outcome
was it?

✓
passed
Matches the expected result.
×
failed
A defect finding — read the evidence.
!
error
A tool or access failure, not a defect.
‖
blocked
A prerequisite or an open gap stopped it.
–
not_run
Not selected, or deferred by the caller.
The outcome is a field, not a sentence.
Which is what lets an agent act on it without guessing.
