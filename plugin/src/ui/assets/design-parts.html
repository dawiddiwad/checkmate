Keys

UpDown Move the highlight through the rows where you are: the plans, the navigator, the records, the files.
RightEnter Go one step deeper: open the plan, enter the list, open the record, view the file. An area of rules expands.
LeftEsc Go one step back: close the viewer, close the record, return to the navigator, return to the plans. An area collapses. Esc first clears a selection.
123…9 Choose an option of the highlighted record. The options are numbered from left to right as shown. When records are selected, the numbers act on the selection, and an action on more than one record asks first. On a rule or an area in the navigator, choose a build column; the same number again shows all builds.
Space Select or unselect the highlighted record, to act on several records at once.
Shift+UpShift+Down Select the records on the way, from the highlighted record.
0 Undo the last hand-over while its note shows. At other times 0 does nothing.
/Ctrl+K Find a plan, a defect, or a question, or ask Checkmate. In the find bar, Up and Down move in the results, Enter opens one, Left and Right move the caret, and Esc closes it.
? Show or close this list of keys.
In a text field
Enter Send a one-line answer, a reason, or a name.
Shift+Enter Start a new line.
Ctrl+Enter Send a larger form, for example a decision on the plan page.
Esc Leave the field. The text stays in the field.
LeftRight Move the caret. The arrows do not move the highlight while you type.

Tab moves between the regions: the top bar, the navigator, the list, the record, and the status bar. Inside a region the arrows move. Each action also works with the mouse.
